# Purpose

This document defines the scope of the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for SymptoSense. It clearly identifies what will be included in the first release, what is planned for future versions, and what is intentionally out of scope.

The purpose of this document is to keep the product focused, prevent scope creep, and ensure that every feature contributes to solving the core problem defined in the Product Vision.

---

# MVP Core Features

These features represent the core functionality required for the first release of SymptoSense.

## Anonymous First Assessment (Guest Mode)

Users can begin their first symptom assessment without creating an account.

During the assessment, the platform collects the basic health information required to provide an accurate evaluation.

After receiving the assessment report, users are encouraged to create an account to save their assessment history and health profile.

---

## User Registration & Login

Registered users can:

- Save assessment history.
- Access previous reports.
- Reuse their health profile.
- Complete future assessments faster.

---

## Interactive 3D Human Body

Users can identify the affected body region using an interactive 3D human body model.

The 3D model serves as one of the primary methods for starting a symptom assessment.

---

## Conversational AI Assessment

Users can naturally describe their symptoms through an AI-powered conversation instead of filling out traditional forms.

The AI guides users through the assessment by asking intelligent follow-up questions based on previous responses.

---

## Voice Input

Users may describe their symptoms using voice instead of typing.

Voice input is automatically converted into text before continuing the assessment.

---

## Dynamic Follow-up Questions

Assessment questions are generated dynamically according to the user's previous answers.

This allows the assessment to become increasingly personalized and medically relevant.

---

## Emergency Detection

The platform detects symptoms that may indicate emergency medical situations.

When emergency indicators are identified, the assessment immediately advises the user to seek urgent medical attention.

---

## Symptom Assessment Report

At the end of every assessment, users receive a structured report containing:

- Possible health conditions.
- Urgency level.
- Recommended next step.
- Recommended medical specialty.
- Medical disclaimer.

---

## Medical Specialty Recommendation

The platform recommends the most appropriate medical specialty based on the assessment results.

Examples include:

- Internal Medicine
- Neurology
- Orthopedics
- Dermatology
- ENT

---

## Assessment History

Registered users can review all previous symptom assessments and reports from their account.

---

## Arabic-First Experience

The first version of SymptoSense is designed primarily for Arabic-speaking users.

This includes:

- Arabic user interface.
- Right-to-left (RTL) support.
- Natural Arabic conversations.
- Initial support for Egyptian Arabic.

---

# Health Context

To improve assessment accuracy, SymptoSense considers the user's health context.

During the first guest assessment, the platform may collect:

- Age
- Gender
- Height (Optional)
- Weight (Optional)
- Chronic Diseases
- Current Medications
- Allergies
- Pregnancy Status (when applicable)

If the user creates an account after the assessment, this information is automatically saved as part of the user's Health Profile and reused in future assessments.

# Future Scope

The following features are planned for future releases but are not part of the MVP.

- Family Accounts.
- Parent Mode.
- Child Symptom Assessment.
- Chronic Disease Follow-up.
- AI Vision (Image-based Symptom Analysis).
- OCR for Medical Reports.
- Wearable Device Integration.
- Apple Health Integration.
- Google Health Connect Integration.
- Multi-language Support.
- Follow-up Reminders.
- Doctor & Clinic Recommendations.
- AI Health Insights based on assessment history.

---

# Out of Scope

The following features are intentionally excluded from the MVP.

- Online doctor consultations.
- Video consultations.
- Doctor appointment booking.
- Medicine prescriptions.
- Online pharmacy services.
- Hospital Management Systems.
- Electronic Medical Record (EMR/EHR) systems.
- Ambulance dispatch services.
- Insurance services.
- Veterinary health assessments.

---

# Scope Principles

Every feature included in the MVP should satisfy at least one of the following objectives:

- Help users understand their symptoms.
- Reduce uncertainty.
- Improve assessment accuracy.
- Guide users toward the appropriate medical specialty.
- Help users determine the correct next medical step.
- Promote safe and responsible healthcare decisions.

Features that do not directly contribute to these objectives should be postponed to future releases.

---

# MVP Goal

The first release of SymptoSense aims to deliver a complete, reliable, and AI-powered symptom assessment experience that helps users understand their symptoms, recognize potential emergency situations, identify the appropriate medical specialty, and confidently decide on their next medical step.
