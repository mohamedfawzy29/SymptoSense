# Purpose

This document defines the primary goals that SymptoSense aims to achieve. These goals guide product decisions, feature prioritization, and long-term development.

---

# User Goals

SymptoSense aims to help users:

- Better understand their symptoms.
- Reduce uncertainty and unnecessary anxiety.
- Know when immediate medical attention may be required.
- Identify the most appropriate medical specialty.
- Make informed decisions about their next medical step.

---

# Product Goals

The product aims to:

- Deliver an intuitive and interactive symptom assessment experience.
- Replace unstructured internet searching with guided assessment.
- Build user trust through transparency and responsible AI.
- Support both new symptoms and symptoms related to chronic conditions.
- Make healthcare guidance more accessible to Arabic-speaking users.

---

# Long-Term Goals

SymptoSense aspires to become:

- The first platform users think of when they experience unfamiliar symptoms.
- The trusted starting point before searching randomly online.
- The leading AI-powered symptom assessment platform in the Arabic-speaking region.

---

# Business Goals

In the future, SymptoSense aims to:

- Build a trusted user community.
- Expand across Arabic-speaking countries.
- Integrate with healthcare providers and digital health services.
- Continuously improve assessment quality using AI and user feedback.

---

# Technical Goals

The platform should be:

- Scalable.
- Secure.
- AI-ready.
- Modular.
- Easy to maintain and extend.

---

# Success Vision

The product will be considered successful when people naturally recommend it in everyday conversations.

For example:

> "I've been feeling pain in my chest for two days and I don't know what's wrong."

Instead of replying:

> "Search on Google."

People reply:

> "Open SymptoSense. It will help you understand your symptoms and tell you what your next step should be."

At that moment, SymptoSense becomes the trusted first step in a user's healthcare journey.
