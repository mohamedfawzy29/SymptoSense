# Purpose

This document defines the intended audience for SymptoSense. Identifying the target users ensures that product decisions, user experience, AI interactions, and future features are designed to solve the right problems for the right people.

---

# Primary Target Users

The first version of SymptoSense is designed for individuals who:

- Are **15 years old or above**.
- Can understand and interact with a conversational AI.
- Are capable of describing their symptoms.
- Want guidance before deciding what medical action to take.
- May or may not have existing chronic medical conditions.

Typical examples include:

- A university student experiencing unexplained headaches.
- An employee suffering from persistent fatigue.
- Someone experiencing sudden abdominal pain.
- A diabetic patient experiencing a new symptom.
- A person with hypertension who notices unusual dizziness.

---

# User Needs

Our primary users need to:

- Understand what their symptoms may indicate.
- Reduce uncertainty and unnecessary anxiety.
- Know whether their symptoms require urgent attention.
- Know which medical specialty they should consult.
- Receive structured guidance instead of random internet search results.

---

# Secondary Target Users

These users are planned for future versions of the product:

- Parents assessing symptoms for their children.
- Family members caring for elderly relatives.
- Users managing assessments for multiple family members.

---

# Out of Scope (MVP)

The first release of SymptoSense will not target:

- Children under 15 years old using the platform independently.
- Veterinary or animal health assessments.
- Healthcare professionals seeking clinical decision support.
- Hospital management or electronic medical record systems.
- Emergency response or ambulance dispatch services.

---

# Product Assumption

The platform assumes that users can provide reasonably accurate information about their symptoms.

The quality of the assessment depends on the quality and completeness of the information provided by the user.

---

# Key Insight

People are not looking for a diagnosis.

They are looking for confidence in deciding what to do next.

SymptoSense is designed to reduce uncertainty, not to replace medical professionals.
